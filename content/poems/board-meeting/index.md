all week i instruct machines
and the machines obey.
models trained, systems shipped,
a hundred thousand downloads, thank you.

then sunday comes, and i stand
in my own kitchen, flour on my hands,
petitioning two cats
for one scrap of respect.

the motion is denied.
it is always denied.
they walk across my counter
like landlords inspecting a tenant.

they say a prophet is never honored
in his own hometown.
nobody mentioned the kitchen.
