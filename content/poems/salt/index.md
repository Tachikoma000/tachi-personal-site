every load i have ever carried
has weighed less at the shoreline.

the sea solves nothing.
it only keeps its oldest promise,
arriving, arriving, arriving,
until the heart gives up
and matches its breathing.

i come to the water the way
the tired come to their mother's house.
no agenda. nothing to prove.
just to sit a while
where something larger is in charge.
