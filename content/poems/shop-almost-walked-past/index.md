they say the best soup hides
behind the humblest door.

the outside said keep walking.
the two stone lions by the door said otherwise,
grinning like they knew something.
i ducked under the faded curtain
and the inside opened like a grandmother's kitchen,
sweet heat, spice climbing the air,
families folded on the floor,
smiling in the silence of serious eating,
bowl after bowl after bowl.

one small white fan swiveled its head,
left, right, left,
a patient elder cooling the sweat
that may have been the broth
or may have been Okinawa.
i never settled which.

the islanders say nuchi gusui.
food is medicine.
i left with a full belly,
cured of a hunger
i had not thought to name.
