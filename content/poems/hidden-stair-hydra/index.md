an island with no engines,
only donkeys keeping the old pace,
and narrow roads that fold and fold
like a letter someone keeps rereading.

i walked alone, which is one way to listen,
and the road, pleased with my attention,
showed me a secret.
a stair that walked straight into the sea.

i sat on the rocks, and the water
took my feet the way family
takes your bag at the door.

i thanked God, but not with many words.
some blessings prefer
that you simply sit in them.
