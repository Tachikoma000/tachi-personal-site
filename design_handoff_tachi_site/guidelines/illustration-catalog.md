# Illustration Catalog

A study of the 50 hand-drawn illustrations in `assets/illustrations/`, and where each belongs on the site.

## The voice of the set
One consistent hand: a wobbly, single-weight black-ink line (brush-pen weight), organic rounded forms, halftone dot-pattern shading, occasional small marks (×, ✳, teardrops, rays). The register sits between Miró, Matisse cut-outs, and Japanese hanko stamps — naive on the surface, contemplative underneath. Literal subjects (sunrise, mountain, lotus, hourglass) share the page with pure abstraction (blobs, waves, swirls). Faces and figures are doodled abstractions — consistent with the "no face" rule.

## Rules of use
- **Spot art, never icons.** An illustration accompanies a text, marking its mood — it never labels a function, button, or nav item.
- **One per view, at most two.** Ma applies. Small (64–120px), generous margins, never inline with running text except as a tailpiece.
- **Ink stays ink.** No recoloring, no accent tinting. On night paper the black ink vanishes — apply `filter: invert(0.88)` (turns ink to cream) via `body[data-hour="night"]` scope; the Spot component should do this automatically.
- **Natural homes:** beside a piece entry (small, left or right of the title block); under a poem as a tailpiece, centered, ~72px; as a book's emblem on the shelf; a single mark near a section heading.
- **Washi shows through.** They are transparent SVGs — place directly on the paper, never in boxes or circles.

## Placement map
- **Arrival** — bird-creature ("ideas are visitors"), or arch-geometric (a threshold). Use sparingly; the ensō leads this page.
- **Writings index** — per-piece emblems chosen by subject (see catalog). Default if unsure: liquid-blob (the ink itself).
- **Poems** — tailpieces: bird-creature, flower-abstract, moon-crescent (night poems), rain-puddle (grief), lotus (stillness).
- **Books / shelf** — one emblem per work. *Handles, Not Halos*: teamwork-people (the Chorus crowd) or castle-tower (the surveilled city).
- **About** — continuous-line (the one-line thread of a life), compass, or gloves (hands; handles not halos).
- **Colophon / footer** — moon-crescent at night, sunrise-waves by day, or hand-heart.
- **Email (QuietField)** — envelope-clover, small, beside the field.
- **Hour affinities** — dawn: sunrise-waves · morning: sun-rays · midday: mountain-peaks · dusk: wave-flow · l'heure bleue: swirl-spiral · night: moon-crescent.

## Catalog

### Skies, water, weather
- **sunrise-waves** — sun rising over rippled water, rays fanning. Hope, beginnings, Monet's *Impression, soleil levant*. → dawn/morning surfaces, poems of beginnings.
- **sun-rays** — radiant eye-sun above a slope. Illumination, revelation. → epiphany essays, midday.
- **moon-crescent** — vesica moon with an inner sparkle. Night, mystery, quiet. → night colophon, nocturnes.
- **rain-puddle** — puddle mid-splash, drops flung outward. Melancholy, cleansing, renewal. → elegiac poems, weather musings.
- **wave-flow** — a meandering horizontal ribbon of water. Current, flow-state. → musings on process; a gentle divider.
- **wave-fluid** — vertical falling squiggle. Water descending, release. → poems of letting go.
- **liquid-blob** — a lively splashed blob. Ink itself; spontaneity. → the act of writing; default Writings emblem.
- **paint-splatter** — controlled splatter. The creative mess. → craft/process essays.
- **bubbles** — three soap bubbles drifting. Lightness, ephemerality. → playful short musings.

### Flora
- **lotus** — lotus crown atop stacked waves. Purity rising from water. → Poems section, stillness.
- **lotus-meditation** — nested lotus mandala. Deep stillness, mantra. → meditation musings.
- **rose** — layered bloom with lips at center. Love, beauty with a voice. → love poems, beauty essays.
- **flower-organic** — blob flower in a hand-drawn frame. Framed, hand-made beauty. → gallery pieces.
- **flower-abstract** — five-petal star flower with dots. Simple joy. → light poems, tailpieces.
- **flower-bloom** — pinwheel bloom, triangle eye at center. Becoming, unfolding. → growth essays.
- **flower-leaf** — flame-shaped leaf with a watching eye. Growth observed. → nature musings.
- **leaf** — wavy leaf holding a heart. Tenderness in nature. → gentle pieces.
- **leaf-symbol** — faceted, geometric leaf. The engineer's leaf — structure in nature. → physics-meets-poetry pieces.

### Mountains & places
- **mountain-peaks** — jagged ridgeline, halftone slopes. Endurance, scale, Hokusai echo. → perseverance essays, midday.
- **mountain-adventure** — peak cradling a vessel. The journey inward. → travel/quest writings.
- **mountain-silhouette** — one soft M-curve. A calm horizon. → quiet dividers, section marks.
- **castle-tower** — three gothic spires with stars. The city, institutions, halos to be refused. → fiction, *Handles, Not Halos* city.
- **arch-geometric** — draped curtain arches. Threshold, entering. → arrival, first pages.
- **construction** — row of scaffold arches. Building, infrastructure. → systems/building essays (Playgrounds, Rig).

### Figures & tenderness
- **mother-child** — mother and child within an arch. Family, shelter. → family poems/essays.
- **hand-heart** — fanned hand holding a heart. Care, giving. → dedications, colophon.
- **gloves** — two gloved hands. Warmth, protection — hands, handles. → about page; the motto made literal.
- **yoga-meditation** — figure folded in a blob. Practice, breath. → practice musings.
- **stick-figure** — simple stacked figure with symbols. The self, plainly. → humility, about.
- **face-doodle** — joyful abstract face. Voice, humor. → funny musings.
- **teamwork-people** — jagged crowd of forms. The many; the Chorus. → *Handles, Not Halos* emblem; society essays.
- **bird-creature** — one-line bird with a watching eye. The visitor; ideas arriving. → arrival epigraph, poem tailpieces.
- **animal-wildlife** — flowing creature among flora. The wild, untamed. → wilder poems.

### Time, craft & signal
- **hourglass** — hourglass melting into a puddle. Impermanence, mono no aware. → time essays, archives.
- **compass** — leaf-shaped compass with rays. Direction, seeking. → the about thread; navigation essays.
- **boat-water** — hull with sail geometry. Passage, voyage. → journey essays.
- **boat-shipbuilding** — hull in drydock beneath an arch. Craft; the making of vessels. → craft essays; the Rig letter.
- **space-rocket** — abstract rocket among orbits. Wonder; *Ad Astra*. → the sign-off; space/tech pieces.
- **radio-waves** — two concentric-ringed eyes. Signal, resonance, transmission. → AI/communication essays; near QuietField.
- **technology-research** — wide eye with rays. Inquiry, the physicist's gaze. → research essays.
- **envelope-clover** — envelope sealed with a clover. Letters, luck. → correspondence, the email field.

- **abstract-shapes** — seed-eye nested in a blob. The seed of an idea. → "What Wants to Be Written."
- **triangle-puzzle** — a face hiding in a triangle blob. The puzzle of self. → identity essays.
- **eye-triangle** — eye within a triangle. Perception, the observer. → physics/observation pieces.
- **burst-energy** — splat with an asterisk core. Spark, insight. → epiphanies, announcements.

- **sai-ninja** — torii-like sai/gate form. Discipline, the sword — tachi itself. → brand mark adjacent; about.
